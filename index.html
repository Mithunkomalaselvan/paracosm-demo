ABOUT

PARACOSM is the solo project of a doctor, musician and lifelong explorer. It's where two worlds meet — the clinical and the chaotic, the real and the imagined.

Blending progressive rock with electronic textures, PARACOSM is a search for meaning in the everyday, and a belief that love sets us free.
